# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards
Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Posting sexual content or violent threats
- Sustained disruption of project spaces (issues, PRs, chats)

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, project discussion, chat channels) and in-person or virtual events affiliated with the project.

## Enforcement
If someone violates the code, maintainers may take actions including:
- Asking the offender to stop
- Removing or editing comments or contributions
- Temporary or permanent banning from the project

## Reporting
To report an incident, contact the maintainers at: **maintainers@example.com**
Please include: a link to the content, a description of what happened, and any relevant context.
